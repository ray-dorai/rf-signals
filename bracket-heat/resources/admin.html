Salespeople pick a technology or a package on the map, and the survey and the address layer show only APs that qualify. A package qualifies at an address when an AP of an allowed technology is predicted at or above that technology's minimum signal (and within its maximum range, if set). These are your rules. The planner predicts signal and line of sight; it does not measure throughput or sector capacity.
